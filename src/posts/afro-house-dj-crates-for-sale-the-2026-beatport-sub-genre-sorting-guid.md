## What are the new Afro House sub-genres on Beatport?

The current split breaks roughly into three functional buckets, whatever Beatport calls them:

- **Warm-up and groove** — Soulful, Afropop, Acid. Lower energy, songful, built for a room still filling up.
- **Vocal and crossover** — Afrobeats, Afro R&B, Alte, Latin House. These are the tracks with hooks, the ones a crowd sings back at you.
- **Peak-time and dark** — Driving, Peak Time, Psy-Techno. Percussion-forward, built for the point in the night where the room is already moving and just needs more.

This matters because **a lot of Afro House DJ packs for sale** online are sold as one flat 20-track dump with no structure. You get home, load the folder, and realize half of it is 9pm music and half of it is 1am music, with no labels telling you which is which.

## How to build an Afro House crate that plays itself

Here's the practical fix, and it's the same logic we build into every crate at TOP DJ CRATES:

1. **Tag by energy, not just by title.** Before a track goes into your Rekordbox collection, decide: is this a warm-up groove, a vocal crossover moment, or a peak-time driver? Use color tags or comment fields — whatever your software lets you abuse for this.
2. **Check the Camelot key on arrival.** Afro House lives in a narrow BPM band, which means **key clash** is the real enemy, not tempo mismatch. Sort compatible keys into sub-folders so harmonic transitions are a drag-and-drop, not a guessing game.
3. **Separate vocal-forward tracks from instrumental tools.** Afro R&B and Alte cuts carry strong top-line vocals — great for a singalong moment, risky to stack back-to-back with another vocal track. Keep a few instrumental Afro House or Afro Tech cuts on hand as a release valve between them.
4. **Keep an acapella or two in reserve.** Afro House crowds respond hard to **acapella-out** moments layered over a driving instrumental. If your pack doesn't include isolated vocals, that's a gap worth filling separately.
5. **Build a "peak-time only" sub-crate.** Pull every Driving, Peak Time, and Psy-Techno-leaning track into its own folder so you're not hunting through warm-up material at 1am.
