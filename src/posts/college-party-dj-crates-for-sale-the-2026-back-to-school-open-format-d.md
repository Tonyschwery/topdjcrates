## How to Build a Back-to-School Crate: A Practical Checklist

1. **Sort by BPM range first.** College sets usually live between 90-100 BPM (hip-hop/R&B) and 126-132 BPM (dance/mashup). Know where your gear shifts happen before you're on stage.
2. **Tag every acapella and instrumental separately.** You'll want to build your own on-the-fly mashups mid-set, and hunting through folders live is how you kill momentum.
3. **Load your transition pack into hot cues, not a separate playlist.** You want risers and fills one pad away, not three clicks deep.
4. **Front-load recognizable hooks.** College crowds reward instant recognition over slow builds — plan your first 30 minutes around guaranteed sing-alongs.
5. **Keep a "reset" folder ready.** When a request kills your flow, you need a banger on standby to get the room back immediately — this is what [DANCEFLOOR WEAPONS](https://topdjcrates.com/music?crate=17) is for.
6. **Check Camelot keys before you stack mashups.** Two great records in the wrong key will fight each other even at matching BPM.
