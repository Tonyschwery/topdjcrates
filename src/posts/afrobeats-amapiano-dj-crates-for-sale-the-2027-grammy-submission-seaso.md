## Why a Grammy category should change what's in your crate

You don't need to care about award shows to care about what they do to requests. Here's the mechanic:

- **Nominee announcement (Nov 16) spikes casual listening.** Non-DJs stream the nominated tracks out of curiosity. Those same people show up at your next gig expecting to hear them.

## Blending Afrobeats, Amapiano and Afro House without killing your flow

The common mistake is treating these as interchangeable just because a Grammy committee treats them that way. They're not. Respect the differences:

- **Amapiano** lives around 112-115 BPM with log-drum sub patterns that need room to breathe — don't crush it under a hard four-on-the-floor kick.
- **Afrobeats** usually sits 100-110 BPM with vocal-forward hooks — your transition points need to land on the hook, not the verse.
- **Afro House** climbs to 118-124 BPM and rewards a longer, percussive blend rather than a quick cut.

A clean route is Afrobeats → Amapiano → Afro House, nudging tempo up gradually rather than jumping straight from a 102 BPM hook into a 122 BPM groove.
