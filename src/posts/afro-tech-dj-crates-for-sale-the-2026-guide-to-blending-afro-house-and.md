## What actually is Afro Tech?

Afro Tech isn't a Beatport dropdown category you'll always find labeled cleanly. It's the mixing zone where Afro House's tribal percussion and soulful vocal chops meet Tech House's four-on-the-floor drive and rolling low end. Practically, that means:

- **BPM**: 120–125, right where Afro House's upper range (115–125) overlaps Tech House's lower range (124–132).
- **Camelot keys**: heavy on minor keys — 8A, 6A, 7A are the most common homes, so key-matching across the two genres is easier than DJs assume.
- **Groove**: four-on-the-floor kicks, but with organic percussion layered instead of straight tech house claps and rides.
- **Vocals**: sparse, repeated phrases rather than full verses — built for looping and layering, not lyric-driven storytelling.

That overlap is exactly why Afro Tech works as a **bridge crate**. It gives you a tempo- and key-matched runway between two of the biggest rooms on any lineup in 2026.

## What to listen for when picking tracks

Not every Afro House or Tech House track earns a spot in this crate. The ones that work have a few things in common: a **clean intro without a long vocal build**, a percussion loop dense enough to layer under whatever's coming next, and a bassline that doesn't clash with a sub-heavy Tech House kick sitting right behind it. Skip anything with a dominant lead synth melody — those are hard to layer and better saved for a solo peak-time moment rather than a transition.
