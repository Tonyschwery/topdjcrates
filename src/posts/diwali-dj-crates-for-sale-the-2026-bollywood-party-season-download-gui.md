## Why Diwali sets are harder than a normal Bollywood night

A regular Bollywood club set can lean almost entirely on current hits. Diwali is different:

- **Multi-generational floors.** Aunties want Kishore Kumar-era classics, cousins want this year's chartbusters, and the dance floor wants a bhangra-to-EDM bridge that doesn't feel like a genre whiplash.
- **Dhol-heavy openers.** Most Diwali events start with a live dhol or a recorded dhol intro before the DJ takes over — your first transition has to match that energy, not undercut it.
- **Family-hall acoustics.** Community centers and banquet halls aren't clubs. You need clean **WAV** files, not compressed MP3s that fall apart on a rented PA.
- **Tempo whiplash.** Bhangra classics often sit around 85-100 BPM felt-double, while current Punjabi and Bollywood club edits run 100-130 BPM. You need pitch-safe versions with clean intro/outro edits already done for you.
