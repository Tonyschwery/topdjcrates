## What counts as "funky house" in 2026?

Forget the purist definition. The 2026 revival blends three lanes into one dancefloor-ready sound:

- **Classic funky house** — filtered disco loops, garage-adjacent swing, vocal hooks built for singalongs.
- **Nu-disco crossover** — Purple Disco Machine-style production, live bass, string stabs.

## BPM and key basics for mixing funky house

Funky house typically sits **122–128 BPM** — slower than tech house, faster than disco house. That puts it in a comfortable pocket for:

- Blending up from disco house (118–122 BPM) without a big pitch jump.
- Blending down into tech house (126–130 BPM) later in the set.
- Layering acapella outs from funky vocal cuts over tech house instrumentals for an easy energy bridge.

On key mixing: funky house tracks lean major-key and vocal-forward, so **Camelot compatibility matters more here than in instrumental tech house** — clashing a filtered vocal hook against an unrelated key is obvious to a dancefloor in a way a percussion loop isn't. Check the Camelot tag before you drop two vocal-heavy funky cuts back to back. If you're not sure, stick to same-key or adjacent-number moves and you'll be safe.
