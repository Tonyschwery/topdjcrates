## Rekordbox and Serato Prep for Game Day Gigs

Tailgate sets move fast and loose, which means your prep needs to be tighter, not looser. Before the first weekend of your booking run:

- **Tag every track by BPM and Camelot key** in Rekordbox or Serato so you can pivot on the fly when the crowd's energy demands a tempo shift.
- **Pre-load acapella-out versions** of your biggest singalong tracks. A crowd chanting a chorus a cappella over a new beat is a moment worth engineering on purpose.
- **Group your crate into hot cues by function** — opener, throwback, mashup, closer — not just by genre. On game day you're reacting in real time, and you don't have time to dig.
- **Carry more than you think you'll need.** A three-hour tailgate slot can stretch to five if the game runs long. Under-stocked crates are how DJs end up replaying the same eight songs twice.
