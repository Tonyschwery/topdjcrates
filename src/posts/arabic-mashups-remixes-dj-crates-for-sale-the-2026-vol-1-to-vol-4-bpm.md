## How do you blend Arabic tracks without killing the groove?

Tempo matching gets you in the door. **Camelot key compatibility** gets you a mix nobody notices. Arabic mashup productions commonly land around 8B, 10A, or 11B depending on the scale used in the original vocal — tag your library on import and sort by key column in Rekordbox before you build a set folder. If two tracks share a key or sit a step apart on the wheel, you can ride a longer blend instead of a quick cut.

For the actual transition:

- Use the **acapella out** on the outgoing track to bridge into the next track's intro
- EQ out competing percussion before the new darbuka pattern enters — two busy rhythm sections fighting is the fastest way to lose a dancefloor
- If the BPM gap between volumes feels too wide, nudge with pitch control rather than forcing a beatmatch that fights the vocal phrasing
