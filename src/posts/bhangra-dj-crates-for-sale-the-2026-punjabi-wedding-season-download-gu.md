## Building the baraat-to-reception set

A Punjabi wedding isn't one set — it's three or four distinct moods stacked back to back, and each one needs its own lane in your crate:

- **Baraat (groom's procession):** high-energy dhol and brass-forward bhangra, loud and percussive, built for an outdoor crowd dancing behind a car or horse.
- **Sangeet:** a mix of choreographed Bollywood numbers, bhangra classics, and current chart hits — this is where families request specific songs, so you need depth, not just the obvious ten tracks everyone already has.
- **Cocktail hour:** dialed-back, melodic Bollywood and organic-leaning tracks that let people talk while still feeling festive.
- **Reception peak time:** this is where bhangra collides with open format — Afrobeats, Latin, hip hop, whatever gets the multigenerational floor moving together.
