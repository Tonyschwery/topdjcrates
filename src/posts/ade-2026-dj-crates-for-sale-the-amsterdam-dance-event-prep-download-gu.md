## The actual prep checklist before you fly

Don't wing this. Here's the workflow that keeps you out of trouble mid-set in an unfamiliar booth:

1. **Audit your crate by BPM range**, not by mood. Afro house at 118-124, tech house at 126-130, melodic house/techno at 122-128. Know your ranges cold before you're standing in front of a CDJ you've never touched.
2. **Tag everything in Camelot keys** before you leave. Harmonic mixing under pressure, in a foreign booth, on gear you don't own, is not the time to guess.
3. **Confirm your format.** WAV, not MP3. European club systems are unforgiving on compressed files, and a bad transcode will embarrass you on a system that size.
4. **Load backups on two devices.** USB failure at ADE isn't a story you want to be in.
5. **Build 2-3 transition tracks per genre pairing** — afro house into tech house, tech house into melodic, melodic into disco. These are the tracks that save your set when the crowd shifts and you need an exit ramp.
