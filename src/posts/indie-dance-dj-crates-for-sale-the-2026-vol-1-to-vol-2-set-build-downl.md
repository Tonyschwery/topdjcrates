## What BPM range should you expect?

Indie dance and dark disco generally sit in a tighter pocket than most four-on-the-floor genres — usually somewhere in the 118-124 BPM range, closer to disco and nu-disco tempos than to tech house. That's actually an advantage. It means you can hold a groove for a long stretch without constant tempo jumps, and it means transitions into funk, disco house, or even slower tech house in your crate box are easier than you'd think.
