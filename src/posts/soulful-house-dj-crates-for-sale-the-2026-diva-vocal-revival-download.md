## What actually counts as "soulful house" right now?

It's not one strict genre tag — it's a feel. Think:

- **Vocal-forward house** built around a hook, not just a sample chop
- **Classic disco and funk chord progressions** run through a modern house kick
- **Handbag house and diva house revivals** pulling from '90s/2000s vocal records
- **Funky house grooves** with live-sounding bass and Rhodes keys

If that description sounds like it overlaps with disco house and funky house, that's because it does. Soulful house in 2026 is less a new lane and more a reclaiming of the vocal, groove-first end of house music that got crowded out by tech house's dominance over the last few years.

## How do you actually mix soulful house into a modern set?

This is where DJs get stuck. Soulful house lives at a tempo range (usually **118-124 BPM**) that sits right between organic house and tech house, which means it's a perfect bridge — if you handle the transition right.

1. **Check the Camelot key before you drop it.** Vocal tracks clash hard when keys fight. Soulful house vocals are the first thing a crowd notices going out of tune.
2. **Use the instrumental or dub mix to ease in.** Drop the vocal on the second or third phrase, not the first beat — let the groove establish itself first.
3. **Pair with a disco or funky house record on either side.** The tempo and swing match naturally, so your transitions don't need hard cuts.
4. **Save the biggest vocal hooks for a clear floor moment.** A strong diva hook works best without competing energy — don't bury it under a peak-time tech house set.
5. **Watch your outro length.** Classic vocal house edits often run long; **intro/outro edits** matter more here than almost any other genre, because you need clean mix points without cutting off a hook mid-phrase.
