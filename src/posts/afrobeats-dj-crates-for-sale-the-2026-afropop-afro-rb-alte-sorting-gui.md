## What's the Difference Between Afropop, Afro R&B, and Alte?

These three sit under the Afrobeats umbrella but behave very differently in a set:

- **Afropop** — the mainstream chart sound. Mid-tempo, hook-driven, built for singalongs. Think the records your open-format crowd already knows the words to.
- **Afro R&B** — slower, vocal-forward, often sub-100 BPM. These are your transition tracks, your "bring the energy down for a minute" tracks, and your first-dance alternates at weddings.
- **Alte** — the Nigerian alternative lane. Looser structure, more experimental production, less chart-chasing. It's the record you drop for a crowd that wants to hear you know more than the top ten.

Three different jobs. One old tag used to lump them together, which meant you were scrolling through fifty tracks to find the one that actually fit the moment in your set.
