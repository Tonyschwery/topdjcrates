Forget the record-collector definition. For set-building purposes, funk in 2026 splits into three lanes:

## How do you actually mix funk into a modern set?

1. **Anchor your transitions in the 115-124 BPM overlap zone.** This is where boogie, disco, and funky house all breathe the same tempo, so pitch adjustments stay small and your groove stays intact.
2. **Use the breakdown, not the drop, as your entry point.** Funk tracks build on groove, not a big EDM-style drop. Bring them in on a breakdown or bridge where the bassline is exposed.
3. **Lean on acapella outs and drum breaks for blends.** A clean acapella lets you layer a funk vocal hook over a disco-house instrumental without fighting two full arrangements.
4. **Check Camelot keys before you layer anything.** Funk basslines are loud in the mix — a clash here is obvious to everyone on the floor, not just DJs.
5. **Keep one funk-forward set ready for the warm-up hour.** Bookers are asking for it more in 2026, especially for daytime and terrace events where a straight techno warm-up feels wrong.
