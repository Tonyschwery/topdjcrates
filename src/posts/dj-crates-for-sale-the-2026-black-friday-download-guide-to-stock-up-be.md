## How to build your Black Friday crate list before the rush

Don't wait until the week of to figure out what you actually need. Do this now:

1. **Pull up your last 10 setlists.** Which genres kept coming back? Those are your priority buys.
2. **Check your library for dead weight.** Old, low-bitrate MP3s from a 2019 blog download don't belong next to new WAV crates. Flag them for replacement.
3. **Match crates to your next six weeks of bookings.** A wedding-heavy November needs different tools than a club-residency November.
4. **Buy in genre blocks, not single tracks.** A crate with consistent BPM and key tagging saves you real prep time versus hunting for one-off singles across five different stores.
5. **Load and test before game day.** Drop new WAV files into Rekordbox or Serato, confirm the tags imported clean, and run a quick blend test. Don't discover a bad tag mid-set.
6. **Back up everything.** A crate you paid for and never backed up is a crate you'll be re-downloading at the worst possible moment.

## WAV vs. MP3: why format still decides the sale

This comes up every time someone asks why a "free pack" from a random forum sounds thin on a club system. MP3 compression throws away audio data to shrink file size. On earbuds, you won't notice. On a 15k rig or even a decent pair of active monitors, you will. **WAV keeps the full file intact** — the low end, the transients, the detail that makes a drop actually hit.

When you're comparing **DJ crates for sale** from different sources, format is the first filter, not the last. If a seller doesn't specify WAV, assume it's not.
