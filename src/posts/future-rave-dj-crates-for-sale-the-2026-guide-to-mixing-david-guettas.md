## How to mix future rave into a tech house or electro house set

1. **Build your BPM bridge first.** Start your transition two tracks out. Move from your current tempo toward 126–128 BPM gradually — don't jump straight into a future rave track from a 122 BPM afro tech groove.
2. **Use the breakdown, not the drop, as your entry point.** Future rave tracks have long, exposed breakdowns built for mixing. Bring your incoming track in during that section, not cold on the drop.
3. **Match Camelot keys on the blend.** A clash here is obvious fast — the supersaw leads expose bad key matches harder than a deep house bassline ever will.
4. **Ride the riser with your EQ, not your fader.** Pull highs on the outgoing track as the riser builds on the incoming one. Let the drop land clean and full.
5. **Keep an intro/outro edit on standby.** If the raw track has a long intro with no beat, you need a pre-edited version with drums up front to avoid dead air on a packed floor. This is exactly what a well-cut **WAV intro edit** is for.
